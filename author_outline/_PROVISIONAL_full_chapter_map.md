> **Provisional. Nothing locked, nothing redone.** This is the *re-break* of the 30-chapter / 5-act outline into the **Full** version: overloaded chapters split, satellite arcs promoted to their own chapters, **interwoven** (cut between POVs in reading order — not a walled-off "Scattering" section). > **Map tags:** **[split]** = pulled out of an overloaded chapter · **[promoted]** = was an interlude · **[new]** = new placement.
