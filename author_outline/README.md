# Dominion Realm — Author's Reading Outline

**This folder is yours.** It's the whole book in plain language — chapter by chapter, scene by scene, with the beats laid out to read and write against. It is *not* the agent planning layer (`scene_queue.md`, `book1_chapter_plan.md`, the briefs). Those are working files with IDs, gates, and tags; this is the narrative view, written to be read.

**How to read the tags** (kept light on purpose):
- **(locked)** — an invariant; the prose can't contradict it (these trace back to `relationship_invariants.md` etc.).
- **(proposed)** — a scene or beat I've proposed to fill a gap; yours to approve, change, or cut.
- **(to decide)** — an open choice that affects this scene.

Canon governs. Where the old prose draft or the `.docx` disagrees with what's here, this is right and they're stale.

## The promises this outline keeps (the spine under everything)
- **Marcus & Serra:** equals from the start (the duel *reveals* that, never earns it); the charge is real but stays under the surface until the end; recognition is staggered; the book ends on the **severance** — she cuts the thread (partly to free him), he doesn't follow.
- **The Six scatter and are changed by separate roads;** Book 1's question is whether they're still people who belong together.
- **No satellite arc steals the finale's firsts:** Mathias only *senses* the Xyloryn; the Myrmidon is the first living breach. Serra's interface stays dormant until the finale. Seb's power comes after grief. Mara works socially before her interface names itself. Brent is useful before he's powerful. Marcus perceives before he understands what perceiving costs.
