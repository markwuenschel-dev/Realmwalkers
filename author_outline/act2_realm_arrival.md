## Ch 5 — The Bargain
*Marcus · Realm Day 1 (internal time)*

**What happens:** The mindscape turns from lesson to ordeal — an archdemon, archangels, torture, and a bargain Marcus wins at the price of his life. He gets a powerful, dangerous item and no protection, and then he dies.

**Scenes / beats:**
- The archdemon **Xazzidiuk** erupts; archangels contain it; Marcus is wounded, then healed.
- The prison fails; a dual-possession attempt; mindscape torture.
- **Marcus wins the boulder bargain.** Xazzidiuk's vow grants the **Eyes of Meszkhal** — a Unique item (this is the *item*, separate from the native eye-interface that emerges later). The archangel **Zazriel is present and gives him nothing** — no protection, no fortitude. (locked — Zazriel withholds)

## Ch 6 — The Light
*Marcus · Realm Days 1–3*

**What happens:** After death, a void, an approaching light, and a resurrection on new ground — anchored by Ayla, who attaches herself to him on terms. He takes stock, and chooses to stay himself.

**Scenes / beats:**

- **Day 3:** he wakes in new terrain. **Ayla** negotiates attachment — the deal is struck. (She's a bounded presence — a guide with limits and damage, not an answer-machine.)
