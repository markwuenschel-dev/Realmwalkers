01

Executive summary

Realmwalkers remains a well-built, unusually self-aware codebase — clean static surface (ruff + format zero-issue at HEAD), disciplined fixtures, and real parity tests where they exist. Since 2026-07-17 the team resolved 6 of the prior 26 candidates (the three confirmed bugs plus the doc/CI/dead-flag quick wins), all verified against commits. This run adds 12 fresh candidates and re-scores the 12 that carried. Three themes dominate.

1. An approval-ledger hotspot with four interlocking bugs. One subsystem — reviews.decide → memory/ledger.py → character_state — now carries four distinct defects that compound. The headline is new and high-severity: CHAR-UNIQ — character_state has no UNIQUE(book_id, character) constraint (verified: the model has no __table_args__ at all), yet its docstring asserts one row per key and 5+ readers use scalar_one_or_none; a single case-variant name or concurrent first-approval inserts a duplicate that permanently 500s every Oracle/ledger/world read with MultipleResultsFound. On top of it sit LEDGER (relative +N deltas double-apply on every revise→re-approve), DECIDE-LOCK (the approval path lacks the with_for_update the repair path was explicitly hardened with), and KNOW-MONO (the reader-knowledge marker is last-writer-wins, not monotonic). These reach future generation through the Oracle, not an inert column.

2. Hand-maintained mirrors and stringly-typed contracts, guarded only by comments — unchanged since last run. ~40 enum vocabularies persist as bare Text across 84 columns with no SA Enum/CHECK (ENUM-STR); artifact_type is free text with the literal scene_fidelity_report triplicated across three modules (ARTIFACT-TYPE); a 22-slug SECTION_ORDER and the ChapterKind list are hand-mirrored across the Python/TypeScript boundary (SECTION-MIRROR); the chapter-band ordering law is re-typed in SQL (ORDER-BACKFILL). All were verified aligned today — the risk is entirely silent drift-under-change, and every one converts to a cheap parity test.

3. Verification blind spots persist and grew. There is still no HTTP-level harness (all 27 routers tested as plain functions — HTTP-HARNESS), and this run adds two more: TEST-DB-SKIP — the everyday just test command omits DOMINION_REQUIRE_DB, so a Postgres-down run silently skips 61 of 119 test files and reports green (verified in conftest.py:139) — and VERIFY-GATE, where local verify still claims CI-parity while type-checking a narrower scope. Three candidates are genuine human-decision forks — PROD-FACADE (complete vs dissolve the facade), ENUM-STR (SA Enum vs CHECK vs app-test migration), and AUTHZ-AXIS (the A1c authorization-vs-blast-radius split) — and are marked needs-decision.

Coverage honesty. This is a 4-lane sampling swarm, not an exhaustive re-scan. Eight prior-open candidates were not re-surfaced this run (TWIN-PARITY, IMP-EVID, CTRL-PLANE, PKT-PIPE, BEAT-NOUN, TELEM-AGG, MIG-DRIFT, ADR28-SUB). They are not treated as resolved — only the 6 with commit evidence are. See the carried-forward panel below the ledger.

06

Lane findings & health

Lane 1 — Deepening seams (C). The production trio is the structural weak point. workers/production.py presents itself as the public facade yet the router imports production_repair/production_support directly for fidelity previews and update_run_summary, and re-exports private helpers with zero consumers — it fails its own stated contract. production_repair.py bundles three concerns (repair lifecycle, fidelity triage, author previews); its two verify paths duplicate ~120 lines that can drift; authority_level is a Text column overloading blast-radius with authorization-requirement. Fed: PROD-FACADE, REPAIR-GOD, VERIFY-DUP, DEAD-BRANCH, AUTHZ-AXIS, ENUM-STR, LLM-SIG.

Lane 2 — Graded integrity (B-). Concurrency and performance are genuinely solid; the findings cluster in one subsystem — the CharacterState ledger and its knowledge sibling. The strongest is a schema-contract violation (CHAR-UNIQ) that permanently 500s core reads once any duplicate row exists; two double-apply bugs sit on top (LEDGER's resettable-status guard, DECIDE-LOCK's missing row lock). Plus the sweeper's failure cap only counts successes and the knowledge marker is last-writer-wins. Fed: CHAR-UNIQ, LEDGER, DECIDE-LOCK, SWEEPER-CAP, KNOW-MONO, SEV-ALIAS.

Lane 3 — Blast radius (C+). The dominant pattern: a vocabulary or ordering law authored once, then re-encoded as bare Text columns, per-module string constants, cross-language mirror lists, or copy-pasted predicates — with a KEEP IN SYNC comment standing in for a check. Verified aligned today (SECTION_ORDER identical, migration bands reproduce chapter_position), so the risk is future drift, not present breakage. Fed: ENUM-STR, ARTIFACT-TYPE, SEV-ALIAS, SECTION-MIRROR, ORDER-BACKFILL, CANON-STATUS, FE-TYPES-DRIFT.

Lane 4 — Verification readiness (C+). Disciplined where it exists, but one structural hole dominates: no HTTP-level harness at any tier, so FastAPI routing, DI, request validation, CORS, and the lifespan boot have zero red-capable check; the sole e2e boots the frontend with no backend. New this run: the default just test silently skips 61/119 files when Postgres is down, and local verify's pyright scope no longer matches CI. Fed: HTTP-HARNESS, TEST-DB-SKIP, VERIFY-GATE, E2E-GATE, CH1-GOLD, TELEM-ORACLE.

Cross-lane duplicate groups

approval-ledger-hotspot — CHAR-UNIQ, LEDGER, DECIDE-LOCK, and KNOW-MONO all live at the reviews.decide → memory/ledger.py/knowledge.py → character_state seam. Kept as four candidates because the fixes are genuinely different (a unique constraint + upsert; a durable applied-marker; a with_for_update read; a monotonic-min assignment). They share a root theme — one-shot side effects gated by resettable/last-writer state rather than durable markers — and are best drained together.

stringly-typed-enums — ENUM-STR (blast-radius: 84 Text columns, no SA Enum/CHECK) and the deepening-seams authority_level .value-inconsistency merged into ENUM-STR as one systemic candidate; the authority_level comparisons are the worked example. Distinct from AUTHZ-AXIS, which is the semantic overload of that same column (blast-radius vs authorization-requirement), not its typing.

severity-vocabulary — graded-integrity's "is-blocking hand-rolled ~18×" and blast-radius's "two parallel severity vocabularies + legacy hard alias + token collision" describe one gap; merged into SEV-ALIAS. The cross-lane agreement raises its confidence to 5.

http-integration-gap — HTTP-HARNESS (no ASGI harness) and E2E-GATE (backendless, full-tier-only e2e) are the same untested frontend↔backend boundary from two ends; kept separate because one adds a fixture and the other re-scopes a CI tier. HTTP-HARNESS is the unblocking prerequisite.

12

Verification readiness

The testability picture is again the report's most consequential axis. Harnesses that must exist before common fixes can be proven:

  • An ASGI client fixture (httpx.AsyncClient(transport=ASGITransport(app=app)), zero new deps — httpx>=0.27 is already a dependency). Without it, no router fix — nor the ~120-line lifespan boot in main.py — can be verified end-to-end. → HTTP-HARNESS.
  • A loud default test command. just test silently skips 61/119 files with Postgres down; making it export DOMINION_REQUIRE_DB=1 (or fail on a zero-DB-test run) closes the falsely-green everyday loop. → TEST-DB-SKIP (highest-priority, highest-locality item this run).
  • A ch1-golden schema-validation pass — the goldens are consumed as raw dicts and never model_validate'd, so a schema change leaves the regression suite green against a shape production no longer emits. → CH1-GOLD.

Two verification claims are weaker than they read: local just verify type-checks changed files while CI type-checks the full tree (VERIFY-GATE), and the telemetry parity test oracles against helpers from the very module it verifies, so a shared aggregation bug passes (TELEM-ORACLE). Fixture discipline is otherwise good — a versioned SceneFidelity corpus with a structural validator, and ch1 tests hardened to ERROR (not skip) when a lane module is missing.
