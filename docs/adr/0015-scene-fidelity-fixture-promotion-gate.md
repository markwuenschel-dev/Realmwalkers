# SceneFidelity fixture promotion gate

No SceneFidelity model, prompt, fallback, schema, merger, or policy change is promotable without the versioned fixture corpus. Hard fixtures must pass exactly; judgment fixtures require reviewed false-positive and false-negative deltas; exploratory fixtures remain non-blocking. Fallback eligibility requires live-corpus quality evidence, and each promotion records the change, corpus version, outcomes, deltas, and human rationale.
