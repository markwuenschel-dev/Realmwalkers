# SceneFidelity evidence threshold

Actionable SceneFidelity findings cite clause identifiers, a finding kind, evidence anchors, an explanation, and a surgical correction. Direct contradictions may create repair work when their cited prose is valid; omissions and ambiguities remain warnings until independently corroborated. Omission evidence anchors the nearest expected-beat or scene-transition span and includes the full-prose hash because absence itself has no quote.
