# SceneFidelity refine and replace identity

The author explicitly chooses refine or replace for Fidelity Requirements and Clauses. Refinement preserves identity only for non-semantic clarification; replacement mints new IDs and requires dependency review. Mode, post-draft policy, enforcement, criterion meaning, responsible actor, and required outcome changes require replacement. All edits stale reports, while old Issues and overrides remain attached to their original identities and never transfer silently.
