# Soren — Character (canon)

## Identity
- **Realm name:** Soren (chosen by him in the Realm). **Earth name:** Marcus.
- **True name:** dangerous and hidden; unassigned in canon; usable for metaphysical control.
- **Role:** protagonist and primary narrative lens; Ocular Interface user; settlement-builder;
  Eriadne anchor.
- **Earth context:** core, leadership-adjacent strategic/systems member of a top-25 Aetherfall guild.
- **Current state:** newly arrived in the Realm, en route to / arriving at Eriadne; allegiance
  forming.

## Who he is
Soren is a principled, high-agency protector-builder. His deep drives are integrity, competence,
responsibility, meaning, and the belief that intelligent effort should be able to improve reality. He
is not driven by dominance, status, or winning for its own sake — he wants to make things work, reduce
preventable harm, understand what is actually happening, and still respect himself afterward. He wants
warmth first and understanding second, and because warmth is rare and meaningful to him he can
over-weight it too quickly. His central tension is not reason vs. emotion but the **same cognitive
machinery used for opposite purposes**: regulated, his association, precision, self-audit, and agency
clarify, teach, build, and connect; dysregulated, they become rumination, litigation,
self-prosecution, scorekeeping, and control.

> **Thematic sentence:** a principled, high-agency protector-builder who believes effort, clarity, and
> courage should move reality; when resourced he creates safety, meaning, and connection; when
> overloaded, ambiguity becomes threat, helplessness becomes control, and perceived moral failure
> becomes identity collapse — until he can remain worthy and connected without forcing certainty,
> outcomes, or reciprocity.

## Psychology
**Strengths:** high agency / builder energy; strong moral spine; diagnostic intelligence (finds where
a model broke); honest self-audit; systems intelligence; depth and sincerity; sensitivity to
asymmetry; specific recognition; resilient procedural competence under concrete pressure.

**Vulnerabilities:** helplessness intolerance (tolerates difficulty better than what cannot be
changed); fragile self-trust after a misread; warmth hunger; recognition conflict (wants to be seen
accurately, distrusts generous praise); calibration lag; outcome-bonded worth.

**Failure mode:** under isolation, shame, or an uncontrollable problem his best traits overshoot —
agency becomes control, builder energy becomes solving the system instead of living his life, moral
spine becomes moral injury and impossible purity, self-audit becomes adversarial self-prosecution,
ambiguity becomes threat. His most dangerous collapse is **identity contamination**: not "I made a
mistake" but "I am the mistake."

**The operator without a self:** under prolonged pressure he reduces himself to the component that
still functions — managing supplies, injuries, routes, timelines — treating grief, rest, loneliness,
and desire as less legitimate than what can be organized. The competence is real and is also armor;
others see him functioning and assume he is fine.

**His wound** converges three fears: *I cannot influence what matters; I did not understand what was
happening; what this reveals about me may make me unworthy of connection.* He wants to prove he can be
trusted with power and intimacy without becoming controlling, and be genuinely connected without
losing himself or earning it through endless usefulness.

## Power system — Eyes of Meszkhal
Also called the **Neurochromatic Eyes** (mechanistic) and **Prism Coherence** (final mastered state).
Origin: a mindscape bargain with the archdemon **Xazzidiuk**, fulfilled by killing Soren, who
resurrected. Not demonic in nature; what the Eyes truly are is unresolved.

**Core principle:** the eye does not create power from nothing — it **redistributes bandwidth**.
Emotional state determines what body and mind prioritize. Pupil = aperture, iris = filter, limbal
ring = warning light. Until mastery he cannot turn the eyes off — only suppress, redirect, or
overdrive them.

**Emotional states (boost / cost):**

| State | Boosts | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| Rage | force commitment, pain suppression, target fixation | peripheral awareness, restraint |
| Fear | reflexes, threat detection, escape vectors | decisiveness, stability |
| Calm | precision, efficiency, clean perception | explosive output |
| Obsession | focus, endurance, pattern lock | adaptability |
| Empathy | microexpression reading, emotional prediction | self-protection |
| Grief | persistence, pain tolerance, emotional depth | initiative |

**Progression:** 0 Latent Human Eye → 1 Limbal Shift (emotion leaks at the iris edge) → 2 Iris
Refraction (active perception) → 3 Neuro-Optical Overdrive (strains the eye to amplify the current
state) → 4 Spectral Partition (separates emotional channels instead of being ruled by one) → 5 Gaze
Interference (perception becomes bidirectional, affecting others through eye contact) → Final Prism
Coherence (full control, efficient activation, subtle baseline enhancement, emotional integration).

**Abilities:** emotional leakage (a tell at low levels, a controlled signal at high); microexpression
reading; **copycat combat** (copies movement by reading weight transfer, muscle sequencing, timing,
intent, and recovery windows — cannot copy strength, anatomy, magic bonds, or nonhuman biomechanics);
time dilation (perception density under stress, not literal); mesmer / gaze interference (advanced —
emotional entrainment, fascination, attention capture, hesitation).

**Cost & risk:** finite ocular reserve (oxygen, glucose, neurotransmitters, vascular tolerance, fiber
integrity). Overuse causes eye burning, blurred color, migraines, blood tears, broken vessels, damaged
fibers, and permanent distortion if abused. **Biggest danger — emotional feedback lock:** the eye
amplifies a state, the state changes perception, perception reinforces the state, the loop tightens.

**Visual signature:** chromatic limbal ring; opalescent, oil-slick iris sheen with flowing gradients;
color shifting with emotion; at overdrive, visible veins around the eye and blood tears.

**Thematic resonance:** his powers externalize his psychology — he sees patterns, sees too much, learns
by observation, is vulnerable to false certainty, and is tempted by anything that promises to remove
ambiguity.

**Temptations** (his are certainty, absolution, and control — not greed): "Let me show you what they
really think." / "Let me remove ambiguity." / "Let me make people readable." / "Let me make rejection
impossible." / "Let me prove you are good." / "Let me guarantee you never hurt anyone again."

## Relationships
- **Serra (Earth name Sarah Hawthorne):** an unaddressed Earth moment left things charged. In the
  Realm they find each other early, survive something together, and separate when she makes a call he
  understands but cannot follow. He reads her accurately on reunion and ruminates; she has already
  decided. He wants warmth without conditions; she gives warmth freely but not the inside. *Currently
  separated.*
- **Sebastion "Seb" Rainier:** mirror-rival; the same high-agency, helplessness-fearing drive
  expressed oppositely — Seb chooses control, Soren must learn connection.
- **Ayla:** the celestial voice in his head (attached after his first death); mentor, unreliable and
  incomplete; sharpens his questions rather than answering them.
- **Mara Valeria:** challenges his tendency to treat systems as puzzles rather than communities —
  people are not resources or variables.
- **Mathias Sterling:** challenges his build-instinct — asks what the land can survive, not just what
  the settlement needs.
- **Brent Donovan:** grounds him; turns abstraction into practical constraints when he is trapped in
  analysis.

## Arc (Book 1)
Primary lens. Arrives in the Realm, survives the implant activation, gains the Eyes through the
Xazzidiuk bargain, dies and resurrects, meets Ayla (the archangel Zazriel was present but gave
nothing). Moves toward Eriadne, forms a fragile base in a ruined district nearby, becomes politically
relevant to its conflict. Starts as survivor, analyst, and builder — alone, just back from death, eyes
beginning to change, building a map toward a city he has never seen. Ends tied to Eriadne — essential,
not in control; the old guild identity dead; the new question whether these changed, powerful people
can align without becoming enemies.

> **Arc question:** when to trust what he sees, when to question it, and when to stop looking and
> actually connect.

## Canonical anchor
The paladin vs. blood mage encounter — its structure, visual detail, and the blood mage's
self-sacrifice — originates from the author's own writing and is a fixed canon anchor.

## Unresolved (do not invent)
