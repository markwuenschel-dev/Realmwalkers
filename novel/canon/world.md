# Dominion Realm — World & Systems (canon)

## The Realm and Earth
The principal characters come from Earth, where they were elite players in a competitive system
called **Aetherfall**. They are brought into **the Realm** — a real world, not a game. The game-like
interface each of them perceives (status text, levels, system readouts) is their own mind processing
a real place; it is an interpretation layer, not the truth of the world. The implant each carries is
a **translation layer, not a power source**.

## Astria and the implants
**Astria** is the organization behind the offers that brought them over and behind the implants. The
implant was secretly designed by nonhuman knowledge to receive Realm-interface magic, and Astria's
upper leadership was deceived or influenced into deploying it. The characters do not know this yet.

## Power interfaces
Each transported person develops a biological **interface** in the Realm — an emotion- and
perception-linked system that externalizes the user's psychology and grows in stages. Three are
documented:
- **Soren — the Eyes of Meszkhal** (perception): he sees the weak point.
- **Serra — the Pressure-Severance Interface** (force): she makes the weak point fail.
- **Mara — Mirror-Salience Transduction** (attention): she authors what a room notices.

Soren's and Serra's systems are an explicit, intentional parallel — *he sees the weak point; she
makes it fail* — complementary, and occasionally catastrophic in combination.

## Eriadne
The city the story converges on. Soren travels toward it, forms a fragile base or community in a
ruined district near it, and becomes politically relevant to Eriadne's larger conflict. Book 1 ends
with him essential to Eriadne without controlling it.

## Cosmology and the mindscape
Power and survival in the Realm can run through a **mindscape** — a metaphysical space where bargains
are struck. Known beings:
- **Xazzidiuk** — an archdemon. Soren gained the Eyes of Meszkhal through a mindscape bargain with
  him; the vow was fulfilled by killing Soren, who then resurrected. The Eyes are *not* framed as
  demonic in nature — their origin is the deal, not a metaphysical category. What they truly are is an
  open question.
- **Zazriel** — an archangel present at Soren's mindscape event who gave no boon: impassive,
  indifferent to a mortal's survival. The long-term meaning of his presence is unresolved; he gave
  nothing.
- **Ayla** — an ancient celestial consciousness that attached to Soren's implant after his first
  death. She is a mentor voice in his head, unreliable and incomplete, who sharpens his questions
  rather than handing him answers.

Death and resurrection are real: Soren has died once and come back.

## True names
A person's **true name** is dangerous and hidden, and can be used for metaphysical control. True
names are not assigned casually.

## Retired / not canon
"Angelic Fortitude" does not exist (removed from canon). Earlier story-bible identities have been
retired (for example, a geneticist / community-activist version of Mara).
