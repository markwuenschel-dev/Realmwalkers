## Core Identity

Kip is forward pressure made into a person. He is the squad's frontline warrior — broad even by frontline standards — and his temperament is impatience in the productive sense: he would rather act and adjust than wait and read. Where Mathias studies the room, Kip wants to hit it. He is the foil to the measured scout: direct, confident, allergic to standing still, and inclined to trust the squad's superiority over the enemy's intentions.

## SCENE-001 — First Appearance (Earth canon)

Kip is present at the pre-Worlds scrim against Dead Hand, one of the four 404 squad members Marcus clocks (Seb, Brent, Mathias, Kip). His voice is impatient, direct, and confident:

> "Maybe their internet's bad… Maybe we stop reading their tea leaves and remember we're better than them. We swarm the casters, we collapse the line, we go home twenty-five and zero on the night."

He is "broad even by frontline standards, already shifting his weight from foot to foot like the fight was a held breath he was tired of holding. Kip didn't do waiting." He's the one who names Marcus the "secret weapon" out loud, and as the countdown burns down even he "sounded like he'd swallowed some of the room's stillness" — the forward-pressure character feeling the gravity of facing the best killers in the bracket.
