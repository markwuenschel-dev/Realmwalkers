## Physical / Appearance

> *First impression: ancient damaged guide / celestial remnant.*

Ayla does not need a stable human form unless she chooses one. Default: ancient, partial, not fully embodied — a presence attached to Marcus's implant/core, **voice first, image second.** When visualized, she can appear as a small celestial wisp, a luminous fragment, or a woman-shaped suggestion made from star-pale light and broken memory.

Her "body" looks incomplete on purpose: edges missing, hair or robes behaving like mist, eyes too old for the face, a faint halo of damaged geometry like a shattered icon trying to remember its original shape. Beautiful only the way old ruins, moonlit statues, or half-buried temples are beautiful — not sensual, not cute, not fully safe.

When speaking casually she's dry, sharp, exasperated. When her ancient nature surfaces, the visual turns colder and stranger: white-gold light, celestial script, broken or winglike geometry, a sense that she used to be much larger than the fragment Marcus knows.
