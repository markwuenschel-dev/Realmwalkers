# Mara Valeria — Character (canon)

## Identity
- **Name:** Mara Valeria (Earth and Realm).
- **Role:** a solo arc in Book 1, separated from the guild and from Serra; Mirror-Salience
  Transduction user; Realm class **Spellblade**.
- **Earth context:** a creative professional (design / writing / art — specifics TBD); came up through
  Aetherfall as a setup/precision **Rogue** in tandem with Sarah Hawthorne (Mara sets up, Sarah
  enters). Accepted an offer from **Astria** (her specific motivation is TBD).

## Who she is
Mara does not want invisibility; she wants **freedom from being seen incorrectly**. Attention has
often reduced her to appearance, mystery, or usefulness, so she protects herself through controlled
disclosure and careful management of interpretation. She wants recognition — but specific, earned,
**process-level** recognition (that someone understood *how and why* she decided), not generic
admiration. Her preference order: accurate private recognition > accurate public recognition > useful
invisibility > inaccurate visibility. Her insight: **attention does not just reveal reality, it
organizes it** — what people notice determines who gets help, who is blamed, which danger feels urgent,
whose pain becomes real.

> **Thematic sentence:** Mara wants to be seen accurately more than seen often; when resourced she
> notices what a room overlooks and redirects attention toward what matters; when threatened, privacy
> becomes concealment, perception becomes covert control, and her wish to be understood without
> explaining becomes a test no one knows they are taking.

## Psychology
**Strengths:** controlled self-disclosure; process intelligence (why a decision worked); attention
mapping (who watches whom, what is ignored, which interpretation is winning); specific recognition;
one-on-one depth; restraint; indirect influence; specialized usefulness; cold precision under
misrecognition.

**Failure mode:** privacy → concealment; perceptiveness → covert management of what others notice;
accurate self-definition → intolerance of interpretations she cannot control; competence → dependence
on usefulness as a safe identity; restraint → passivity and missed connection; recognition hunger →
testing whether others can "see" her without being told; anger at misrecognition → relational
disappearance instead of repair. **Core error:** wanting to be understood accurately while withholding
the information that would make accurate understanding possible.

**Hidden weak point:** she fears vulnerability being *misread or exposed* more than vulnerability
itself; competence is her safest route into intimacy because it is a controlled way to be known. Her
danger is believing precise enough management of visibility can prevent misrecognition — it cannot; she
can make a room look at the right fact and still not control what they conclude. **Betrayal**, for her:
"I gave you an accurate piece of myself, and you exposed, distorted, or reduced it." Being chosen
means: *you recognized me without consuming me.*

## Power system — Mirror-Salience Transduction
> *Being seen is not neutral. Attention assigns meaning. Mara learns to author the frame before others
> consume her inside theirs.*

**Core principle:** she does not control minds — she controls what minds **prioritize**. She cannot
implant thoughts, emotions, or decisions; she shifts what rises to the top of awareness (important,
urgent, threatening, present, or irrelevant). Her core power is **authorship of attention**. Core
attribute: **Authorship**. Mechanistically, her body outputs a living "frame" that nearby nervous
systems unconsciously simulate (via mirror-neuron, social-perception, salience, and limbic systems
plus an Aetheric carrier); observers still see reality, but their perception reorders. **Raising**
salience makes a person, threat, or detail feel more urgent and real (sharpens an ally; fixates,
panics, or distracts an enemy); **lowering** fades something out of active priority (their eyes still
see it; their mind does not act on it).

**Progression:** 1 Felt Gaze (senses the shape of attention) → 2 Negative Space (reduces her own
salience; eyes slide past her) → 3 Salience Shift (makes one *real* detail matter; cannot invent
facts) → 4 Reframe (changes how a whole scene is emotionally interpreted) → 5 Witness Field (her frame
becomes environmental; rooms, crowds, and battlefields organize around her authored priority) → **Final
Happy — Sovereign Witness** (visible without being consumed; frames without coercing; gives people back
accurate seeing) **or Final Forbidden — Captive Frame** (authors perception so tightly that others, and
her own unframed self, become trapped).

**Hard limits & costs:** she **cannot make a lie true** — she needs real material and can only make
existing truths easier or harder to ignore. Targets need a sensory channel (machines, undead, alien
minds, and swarms resist). Stable identity resists; people in shame, lust, fear, or panic are easier;
intimates who know the full person behind the frame are protected; over-forcing one interpretation
makes the field brittle and violent. Using it carries **projection load** (absorbing others' desire,
resentment, worship, and contempt — nauseating at scale), **self-image erosion** (losing track of her
unperformed self), sensory overload in crowds, a consent/ethics burden that grows the more morally she
uses it, and **snapback** (a stronger or more coherent will reverses her frame — and Soren might
accidentally read the mechanics of her frame and leave her exposed).

**Thematic resonance:** she spent her life having the wrong things made salient about her; her power is
control of exactly that in others, with a cost calibrated to her wound — used too broadly, she loses
track of who she is when not performing. The transduction was already active in Aetherfall: what she
thought was rogue skill (sliding out of the center of attention) was the power the whole time.

**Realm class — Spellblade:** a class that demands presence and engagement, in deliberate tension with
an interface rooted in disappearing; reconciling "be present" with "watch from the periphery" is part
of her arc. How Spellblade and Mirror-Salience integrate in combat is TBD.

## Relationships
- **Serra (Sarah Hawthorne):** tandem partner, but only as Earth history — they do not connect in the
  Realm before Serra's solo arc begins. Serra reads the emotional field; Mara maps the attention
  structure. Whether the tandem still works once they reunite is unresolved.
- **Soren (Marcus):** no Realm contact yet; his pattern-recognition and her salience-reading will
  overlap (he sees hidden structure; she shifts what feels important) — complementary, or a feedback
  problem. Her standing challenge to him: people are not variables.
- **The cast generally:** she is the one who notices what everyone else is used to not noticing — not
  by analyzing, by watching — and often knows something is wrong before she can say why.

## Arc (Book 1)
Solo arc — separated from the guild and from Serra, developing her power without a framework or a name
for it; she just notices she is harder to see when she wants to be, and that people respond to things
she can trace back to something she did. The arc is discovering what she is capable of where her
appearance no longer precedes her — and finding that the power is intimately tied to the thing she has
most resented about her life. Ends mid-understanding: she has felt the projection load begin to build,
has not lost herself yet, but has had moments unsure what she actually thinks versus what she has been
performing for someone else's salience map; the identity question is becoming urgent.

> **Arc question:** if you spent your life watching people see the wrong things about you, what do you
> become when you can control what they see — and what do you lose in the process?
