# Book 1 — Finale Spine (Act 5 / the Myrmidon + the Severance)

> **Purpose:** the reconciled beat order for the Book-1 finale — the Myrmidon fight and the Marcus/Serra severance, as one continuous movement. Build layer over `master_timeline.md` Act 5 (which owns the chronology) and `book1_chapter_plan.md` Ch 26–30. Precedence: `canon_index.md`.
> **Status tags:** **[LOCKED]** = canon · **[PROPOSED]** = working frame, not locked. The precise physical trigger of the breach is deliberately left flexible (see §A).
> **Structural invariant (LOCKED):** **no main-six character dies in Book 1.** Both Mathias and Brent live; any "others don't make it" beat refers to the captives / the operation, never the six.

---

## A. What forces the portal — [PROPOSED → working frame]

The six's own Book-1 plot (the namebinding / magistrate / trade thread) destabilizes the old convergence lock enough for an outside force to exploit it. The **Nightbound** force/permit a **controlled breach-*test*** — **not** a full invasion — to test whether these candidates survive impossible pressure and whether their deeper interfaces emerge. The six don't know this; the reader only suspects.

The **precise physical trigger is left flexible.** Whether a corpse / N'hal mechanism is what's forced open stays an **unresolved documented option** (W-01, `eriadne.md` / `master_timeline.md`) — **do not lock it.**

---

## B. Beat order — [LOCKED choreography; the trigger in §A stays open]

Reconciled to existing canon: Serra lands the blow · Marcus reads the opening · the eyes ignite · the Meszkhal item lies · high-place severance · Walker recruitment · no-six-dies.

1. Book-1 plot destabilizes the dormant convergence lock.
2. The Nightbound force a controlled breach-test (§A).
3. **One Myrmidon crosses; the swarm stays beyond** — the horror is *this is one.*
4. The six fall back into **Aetherfall plays** (positioning, callouts, tank/healer, burst windows); it feels good — then the Myrmidon breaks the illusion: old plays built for a system they understood.
5. **Mathias** acts on his warning first, is **badly injured because he's right** (not careless); the fight is effectively five-on-one; **he lives.**
6. The **Walker** arrives at the breach, encounters the Nightbound; the exchange is brief and cold; the **Nightbound leaves** once the test has drawn Walker attention; the **Walker stays — not to save the six, but to stop the open door.**
7. **Seb** abandons guild logic, uses Broker-accelerated **Reaver / Threshold Cascade** to anchor the Myrmidon into a focused exchange; losing but not humiliated — power real, cost ominous; buys time.
8. **Brent** sustains everyone (Mathias alive, Seb standing, Marcus/Serra functional); **Mara + Brent** identify the weakness — the Myrmidon is **breach-anchored**; the Walker's sealing pressure + Seb's overcommitment + team timing expose a killable moment.
9. **Marcus** reads the live opening (perception through uncertainty, not clean prediction); **Serra** trusts the read and moves before it's explained — *Marcus sees the line, Serra takes it.*
10. **Serra lands the killing blow.** Severance is *heroic* here — the same capacity that saves everyone later breaks Marcus.
11. The **Walker seals/stabilizes the breach** — the six killed the thing; the Walker closed the war behind it. They are extraordinary and still very small.
12. Victory is real but sobering (don't undercut it): they nearly died against *one.*
13. Peak Marcus/Serra synchronization makes the coming separation impossible to pretend away — he names the connection after *proof*, not failure.
14. **High-place rupture** (their elevated refuge; old rules no longer hold because she's leaving).
15. **Serra severs what she wants back** — feels it, wants it, cuts anyway; tragic, not cold; her one rule **cost-breaks** (`serra_hawthorne.md` → *The One Rule*).
16. **Marcus rejects the Meszkhal item's false simplification** ("if she cuts, the bond was false") — accepts the harder truth: she cares, wants him, leaves anyway.
17. **Both interfaces realize** — Serra's Pressure-Severance because she cuts under full pressure (`serra_hawthorne.md` §2a); Marcus's Neurochromatic Eyes because he perceives contradiction without turning it into ownership. **Interface awakenings = the relationship rupture, same event.**
18. **Serra leaves; Marcus does not follow** — restraint under devastation, not passivity (following would turn perception into claim).
19. **Walker recruitment seed** — the Walker's attention on Marcus becomes explicit; the book ends with Marcus wounded, changed, newly visible to far larger powers.

## Two mechanisms — keep distinct (LOCKED)

- **The Myrmidon "being seen" beat (involuntary):** Marcus's igniting eyes read Serra's *conflict* — his power steals it; the Meszkhal item lies that she's *doubling down*; he reads her right and doesn't hold it against her.
- **The severance "rule-break" (her own act):** Serra cuts the bond *to keep from asking him to stay* — her choice betrays the want. Cost-break, not letter-break.

Two mechanisms; never collapse one into the other. (`serra_hawthorne.md`, `marcus_serra_relationship.md` §8, `relational_clarity_rules.md` RR11.)
