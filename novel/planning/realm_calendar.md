# Realm Calendar — Dominion Realm

> **Status:** Canon
> **Last updated:** working draft

---

## Overview

The Realm does not use Earth calendar systems. Time is measured in a distinct structure of weeks, months, and a year that does not map cleanly to Earth equivalents.

Marcus's implant translates Realm time into a legible format, but early on the calendar means little to him — he is tracking survival, not dates.

---

## The Week — The Forgotten Nine

A Realm week is **9 days**. Their names — the **Forgotten Nine** — are ancient; their original meanings are lost to history, and no scholarly theory (rulers, gods, celestial bodies, lost kingdoms) has ever reached consensus. Most people never question them. They simply *are*.

| Order | Day | Common Association (folklore) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vael | Beginnings, travel, contracts |
| 2 | Kora | Trade, negotiation |
| 3 | Teren | Labor, construction |
| 4 | Ashur | Competition, challenges |
| 5 | Neth | Reflection, learning |
| 6 | Rovan | Markets, festivals |
| 7 | Syth | Family, community |
| 8 | Maelor | Faith, remembrance |
| 9 | Ilyr | Rest, preparation |

The associations are cultural superstition, not universal law — a merchant in Eriadne reads Rovan differently than a frontier farmer.

**Everyday usage.** People drop the word "day": *"The market opens on Rovan." "Meet me on Maelor." "Three Neths ago." "We'll arrive before Ilyr."*

**Formal notation.** *17th of Terramis, Rovan, Year 842* — or *Rovan, 17 Terramis, 842 A.C.* — by regional convention. Years are counted in the **A.C.** era.

## The Year — Months

The Realm year consists of **14 months** of varying length, plus one singular observance day.

| Month | Days | Association |
|---|---|---|
| Solara | 32 | The sun |
| Lunaris | 29 | The moon |
| Aurora | 30 | The Northern Lights |
| Vespera | 31 | Twilight and evening stars |
| Zephyria | 28 | Winds |
| Terramis | 34 | Earth and agriculture |
| Floramis | 27 | Blooming flora |
| Aquanox | 33 | Water and tides |
| Ignatius | 29 | Fire |
| Umbrana | 32 | Shadows |
| Nocturna | 30 | The night |
| Aetheris | 35 | The ethereal and arcane |
| Eclipsia | 1 | Observed during rare solar eclipses only |
| Chronica | 25 | The study of time and history |

**Total standard year:** 400 days (Eclipsia not counted in standard year — occurs rarely)

---

## Year Length vs Earth Year

A Realm year of 400 days ≈ 1.09 Earth years.

Book 1 scope (a few months to three-quarters of a Realm year maximum):
- One Realm month averages ~27–35 days depending on the month
- Three Realm months ≈ ~90–100 days
- Three-quarters of a Realm year ≈ ~300 days

## Narrative Use

### Early story
Marcus does not know the calendar. The interface may display a date but he has no frame of reference. Ayla knows the system but her sense of current date is unreliable — she has been dormant for an unknown period.

### Mid story
Marcus learns month names through contact with locals. Eriadne residents use the calendar naturally. Certain months carry seasonal and political significance.

### Potential story beats tied to calendar

| Month | Possible Significance |
|---|---|
| Terramis | Planting or harvest period; affects food supply and population movement near Eriadne |
| Aetheris | Heightened magical activity; ley-line resonance may intensify near the Eriadne convergence |
| Eclipsia | Single rare day; metaphysical significance unresolved — likely relevant to the ley-line hub |
| Chronica | Dedicated to history; possible significance for the Under-City ruins and the ancient builders |
