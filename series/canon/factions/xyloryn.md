# The Xyloryn

> **Status:** Canon · working draft
> **Type:** Biological assimilating cosmic empire (swarm ecology)
> **Scale:** Cosmic — multi-world invasion; Book 1 reaches the Realm as distant signal, then one living breach
> **Role in series:** The *known* apocalypse — evolutionary pressure made empire; forces the cast to prove the six can act as one unit
> **Canon confidence:** high (deep model + Book 1 finale); medium (origin culprit)
> **Owns:**
> - Xyloryn biology, war loop, Great Genome, essence-wall, Myrmidon rules, mature-world ecology
> **Does not own:**
> - N'hal denaturation (`nhal.md`); ley network scale (`../world/cosmology.md`); who loosed the swarm
> **The spine:** The Xyloryn are not simple hunger — they are an ancient archive of *solved adaptive problems* that observe, consume, diagnose, retrieve, fit, cultivate, and file. They worship pressure. They farm novelty. They can imitate interface surfaces and distill principles from them, but the essence-wall keeps true names, soul-bonds, and divine authority forever outside their reach.

## One-Sentence Identity

A biological swarm-empire that treats worlds as living problem-spaces and archives the answers life reveals under stress.

## Core Principle

> Life must adapt.

Adaptation is sacred — but at empire scale it becomes **forced consumption, retrieval from an ancient library, fitting, cultivation, and archival**: worlds are stressed until life shows something new, then the result is metabolized and stored.

## What They Are Right About

- Pressure reveals what comfort hides; species *do* disclose capacity when survival tightens.
- Adaptation is real, measurable, and often beautiful under constraint.
- A solved problem, once embodied in a caste-form, is a genuine answer — not a costume.

## Where They Go Wrong

- They cannot distinguish *testing* from *ownership* — every world becomes an experiment they are entitled to run to completion.
- Retrieval-plus-fitting feels like live evolution to defenders; it is actually **looking them up** in a library older than empires.
- Cultivation never asks whether the world wanted to become a data point.

## Scale and Reach

- Cosmic-tier swarm spanning many worlds; mature Xyloryn worlds become **swarm ecologies**, not uniform factory planets — special roles emerge from usefulness, not a fixed template.
- Book 1 dosage: **no embodied Xyloryn before the finale** — only signal, trace, portal-pressure, diagnostic skirmishers in planning. The **Myrmidon** is the **first living breach**.
- One Myrmidon should establish scale by **nearly defeating all six together** — group-finale beatable; the swarm itself is not.

## Aesthetic / Texture

- Living fleets, adaptive ecosystems, biological warforms — clinical horror; the body is data; dread accumulates.
- Worlds under cultivation: every niche occupied, nothing comfortable, everything tested.
- Forms read as **readable fossils** — biological answers to old questions (Mara's lane when the swarm arrives).

## Power Model

**War loop:** Observe → Consume → Diagnose → Retrieve → Fit → Cultivate → Archive.

**Three inputs, three timescales:**
1. **Aether** — bulk fuel; funds the impossible numbers (the tide).
2. **Local diagnosis** — fast (days/weeks); scout-bites gather interface/biological data; swarm **retrieves** matching archived solution and tunes fit — not live invention.
3. **The Great Genome** — deep library of **solved problems** (principles + caste-forms), not powers or genes. New entries are rare.

**Surface vs. principle (one spectrum):**
- *Shallow:* crude forgery of interface surfaces (false eyes, spoofed gaze) — carries forgery's weakness.
- *Deep:* distillation — castes self-coherent and **resistant to identity attack** after metabolizing naming-cultures.

> **Essence-wall (load-bearing):** Distilling a principle **never** crosses into operating the essence layer. They may defend against identity attack; they **cannot** wield true names, steal soul-bonds, claim divine authority, anchor ley identity, or hold planar anchors. **They learn to defend against the essence layer, never to operate it.**

Motive and mechanism are the same act: cultivation *is* the experiment.

## Society / Internal Structure

- Not a nation — a **caste ecology** driven by archive retrieval and world-role utility.
- High castes embody ancient answers; low castes graze aether and diagnose.
- No single emperor; coherence emerges from shared archival logic and invasion doctrine.

## Relationship to the Realm

- The **exception among cosmic powers:** they do not rule through proxies — they **invade**.
- The one threat other empires **fear openly**; Realm nations are the board everyone else plays on, but the Xyloryn eat the board.
- Book 1: breach at Eriadne's eight-ley convergence / dormant portal (`../locations/eriadne.md`).

## Relationship to the Six

| Character | Hook |
|---|---|
| Marcus | Crude forms mirror counterfeit certainty; deep forms armored against identity attack but not essence |
| Mara | Every form is a readable fossil — what the swarm has already survived |
| Mathias | Senses distributed-organism pressure; detects ruins-portal at Act 5 finale (injured, survives) |
| Cast (finale) | One Myrmidon tuned to their dominant interfaces — nearly wins; proves reunion can matter |

## Relationship to the N'hal

- **Separate threats.** Xyloryn = biological/interface assimilation; N'hal = ontological denaturation.
- **OPEN:** "Xyloryn as failed immune response to N'hal" — leading hypothesis in story bible, **not confirmed**. Do not write as canon.

## Relationship to Other Powers

- Ideological empires prepare for Xyloryn invasion; none fully trust another's answer.
- Someone may have **loosed** the swarm (broken quarantine, opened route) — culprit **intentionally unresolved**; explicitly **not** whoever brought the six.
- Natural conceptual opposite of the Pale Imperium: they force change; the Imperium refuses loss.

## Story Function

- Visible apocalypse that trains the reader in **scale hierarchy** — the six can beat a Myrmidon, not a civilization-scale wonder.
- Thematic foil to Marcus's arc: counterfeit surface vs. true structure; essence-wall makes the cast's win conditions coherent.
- Book 1 finale owns firsts: first living breach, first time all six must fight as one.

## What They Are Not

- Not the N'hal. Not undead. Not chaos/desire magic. Not demons. Not a simple Zerg/Tyranid hunger clone — they archive **problems**, not loot **powers**.
- Not creators of the six's arrival. Not beatable by refining structure-dependent power alone at cosmic scale.

## Canon Locks

- **LOCK:** War loop = observe → consume → diagnose → retrieve → fit → cultivate → archive.
- **LOCK:** Great Genome stores solved adaptive problems, not power lists.
- **LOCK:** Essence-wall — cannot operate true names, soul-bonds, divine authority, ley identity, planar anchors.
- **LOCK:** Myrmidon = elite shock-form grown/fitted **after** battlefield data; first **living** breach in Book 1.
- **LOCK:** One Myrmidon nearly defeats all six; swarm itself not defeated in Book 1.
- **LOCK:** No embodied Xyloryn before finale; pre-finale = signal/trace/pressure only.
- **LOCK:** Mature worlds = swarm ecologies, not uniform factories.

## Open Questions

- **OPEN:** Who loosed the Xyloryn (rival power, broken quarantine) — culprit stays mystery.
- **OPEN:** Xyloryn origin — ancient/natural vs. engineered (avoid cheap "Empire X made them").
- **OPEN:** Relationship to N'hal — immune-response theory unconfirmed.
- **OPEN:** Who or what forces the Eriadne portal open at finale (W-05).
