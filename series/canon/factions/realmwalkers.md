# Realm Walkers

> **Status:** Canon · working draft
> **Type:** Loose category of cross-realm operators — not a unified organization
> **Scale:** Cross-realm — likely only a few dozen individuals
> **Role in series:** Witnesses who discover, warn, and route — insufficient alone; recruit Marcus late Book 1
> **Canon confidence:** high (identity + Book 1 contact shape); medium ( internal rules)
> **Owns:**
> - Realm Walker category definition, witness ethos, Book 1 Eriadne contact, foil to Nightbound
> **Does not own:**
> - Realmwalker class mechanics (`../litrpg_system/classes.md`); Marcus recruitment arc (`../characters/major/mc.md`)
> **The spine:** Realm Walkers are not a guild or police force — they are a **loose category** of reality-crossing individuals who travel between realms, discover anomalies, witness erased worlds, maintain route customs, and sometimes intervene alone. The core contrast: *The Realm Walkers are witnesses, and witnesses are not enough.*

## One-Sentence Identity

Rare operators who move between interface realities without being fully claimed by one — and argue that seeing the damage should count for more than it does.

## Core Principle

> What is seen must be told.

Witness is sacred — but at scale it becomes **insufficient** — discovery without coordinated preparation leaves worlds to die the same way twice.

## What They Are Right About

- Cross-realm travel reveals damage single-world powers never see.
- Routes, customs, and warnings save lives when honored.
- Balance-keeping matters — no single cosmic philosophy should become total.

## Where They Go Wrong

- Weak enforcement — rules exist, compliance doesn't.
- Individual intervention without coordination repeats Nightbound's critique: too little, too late, too proud.
- May mistake witnessing for action — or action for preparation.

## Scale and Reach

- **Few dozen** individuals — not an army, not a nation.
- Move through and between interface realities without full claim by one philosophy.
- May oppose any single cosmic truth becoming total.

## Aesthetic / Texture

- Travel-worn, route-marked, quiet competence — more pilgrim than knight.
- Mysterious until Book 2 — keep Book 1 contact lean.
- Illyri-adjacent register: old questions, not exposition dumps.

## Power Model

- Cross-realm transit and anomaly reading — not standardized combat doctrine.
- Marcus may qualify: implant/interface reads translation layers others mistake for reality.
- **Realmwalker** (Marcus's Legendary class, one word) is distinct from **the Realm Walkers** / **Realm Walkers** (faction, two words) — class ≠ organization.

## Society / Internal Structure

- **Not unified** — loose category with shared customs, not central command.
- Rules may exist; enforcement is weak.
- One Realm Walker may serve **inside** the Nightbound as pathfinder/evidence-confirmer (preferred over founder-as-former-Walker).

## Relationship to the Realm

- Eriadne's eight-ley convergence holds dormant natural portal (`../locations/eriadne.md`).
- **Book 1 contact (locked):** portal forced open at finale → Myrmidon through → **Realm Walker drawn by breach** → witnesses fight → first contact with Marcus post-Myrmidon — seeds **Realmwalker** class path.

## Relationship to the Six

- **Marcus** — recruited late Book 1; courted by witnesses while already taken by Nightbound interventionists.
- Other six: no locked direct contact Book 1.
- Mathias's stewardship and sensing rhyme with Walker ethos but he is not a Walker.

## Relationship to the N'hal

- Some may have seen denatured worlds — fragmented evidence Nightbound founder also carries.
- Walkers warn; N'hal advance regardless — witness without preparation fails.

## Relationship to Other Powers

- **Nightbound:** ideological foils — witnesses vs. interventionists; mutual contempt, mutual truth.
- Empires: Walkers likely distrust all total answers equally.
- Not hired like Soulkeepers' Exchange — different social contract.

## Story Function

- Marcus late-B1 recruitment; **Realmwalker** class handoff.
- Moral contrast to Nightbound — both critiques valid.
- Mystery preservation until Book 2 deep dive.

## What They Are Not

- Not the Nightbound under another name. Not a formal guild. Not cosmic police. Not unified organization.
- Not enough alone — **by design**.

## Canon Locks

- **LOCK:** Loose category (~few dozen), not unified org.
- **LOCK:** Core contrast — witnesses are not enough.
- **LOCK:** Nightbound think Walkers irresponsible; Walkers think Nightbound dangerous.
- **LOCK:** Book 1 Eriadne breach contact shape — drawn by portal breach, witnesses Myrmidon fight, contacts Marcus after.
- **LOCK:** **Realmwalker** (Marcus's Legendary class, one word) ≠ **the Realm Walkers** / **Realm Walkers** (faction, two words).
- **LOCK:** One Walker may be inside Nightbound as pathfinder.

## Agent Boundaries

- Keep Book 1 Walker presence mysterious and brief.

Agents **must not:**

- Conflate **Realmwalker** (class) with **the Realm Walkers** / **Realm Walkers** (faction) membership automatically.
