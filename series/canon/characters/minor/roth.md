## Core Identity

Roth is institutional power wearing patience as a face. He is the man who arrives when a decision has already been made several rooms away and his job is to make it feel reasonable. His instrument is not threat but inevitability — the sense that the outcome is fixed and he is merely the polite delivery of it.

*Most of his interiority is unestablished. Do not invent psychology beyond the function the scenes give him.*

### Knowledge / Epistemic Status (locked, 2026-07-17)

**Roth believes Astria exploited a genuine medical emergency to accelerate a dangerous but entirely human research program.** That is the whole truth as far as he knows it. He is **not** secretly complicit in anything cosmic — he does not know about the Nightbound, the Broker, the six-key framing, or any external beneficiary. His candor in the Chapter 2 conference room (admitting the hijack, admitting the emergency was used as a recruitment window, never softening the coercion) is **real, not theatrical** — he has nothing further to hide on the topic he's discussing. This is a deliberate author choice: making Roth secretly complicit would be weaker than letting his honesty be genuine. **Do not write him as knowingly serving an off-world agenda.** Compare `leblanc.md` (knows there is a concealed external beneficiary) and `mercer.md` (believes it's an unprecedented but natural neurological phenomenon) — three adjacent, non-overlapping knowledge tiers; see `soulkeepers_exchange.md` → Knowledge Layering.
