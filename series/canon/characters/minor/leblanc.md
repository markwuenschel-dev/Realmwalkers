## Why this file exists now

LeBlanc was already named in `SCENE-001` (the TV announcement) but has no in-person on-page appearance yet, and the earlier plan (`book1_chapter_plan.md` Ch. 3) had him arrive *only* for a private post-surgery reveal scene with Roth. ## Core Identity

LeBlanc is the executive sponsor — the person whose mere presence in the room tells the six that this program reaches higher than either the legal department (Roth) or the medical department (Mercer). He does not run the meeting, doesn't explain the anomaly, and doesn't need to dominate. One or two interventions are enough to make him memorable. His narrative function in Book 1 is proof of scale, not a speaking role.

---

## Knowledge / Epistemic Status (locked, 2026-07-17)

This is LeBlanc's most important story fact, more than anything he says on-page:

**LeBlanc knows the implant program has a concealed external beneficiary.** Specifically: he knows Astria has been preparing the implants for an outside purpose, and that a successful activation will produce some kind of transfer or handoff. He does **not** know about the Nightbound, the six-key framing, the underground seal, or the Broker's true agenda — his knowledge is real but deliberately incomplete, one layer up from Roth and Mercer, not the top of the chain.

**The four-tier structure (locked):**

| Who | Believes / Knows |
|---|---|
| **Mercer** | They discovered an unprecedented, entirely natural neurological phenomenon. |
| **Roth** | Astria exploited a genuine emergency to accelerate a dangerous but human program. |
| **LeBlanc** | The program has a concealed external beneficiary — a transfer/handoff is coming. |
| **The Broker** | Knows more than LeBlanc — but still not everything the Nightbound know (or think they know). |

This preserves the larger mystery (Nightbound / six-key framing / the underground seal stay hidden even from LeBlanc) while giving LeBlanc a real, private, dramatic-irony scene: **after the surgery, the intermediary tells him Astria's portion is complete** — not Roth, who stays genuinely in the dark throughout. See `soulkeepers_exchange.md` → Knowledge Layering for the full chain this slots into (Nightbound → Soulkeepers' Exchange → Broker → Astria → the six).
