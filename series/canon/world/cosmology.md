## The One Truth Each Power Overvalues

| Power | Core truth | Overreach |
|---|---|---|
| **Xyloryn** | Life must adapt | Adaptation → forced consumption and cultivation |
| **The Concord** | Life should improve | Improvement → paternal control |
| **Pale Imperium** | Nothing valuable should be lost | Preservation → possession |
| **Endless Reach** | Desire reveals life | Wanting → sacred enough to override cost |
| **Aurelian Sovereignty** | Reality must be defined to endure | Definition → control over identity |
| **Soulkeepers' Exchange** | Continuity can be transacted | Commerce → half-truth as product |
| **N'hal** | Reality can lose coherence | Not an empire — the condition that makes all other answers inadequate |

Each is *right* about something — which is why people join them, and why the reader can't dismiss any as "the bad guys."

### How they touch the Realm

The four ideological empires (Concord, Pale Imperium, Endless Reach, Aurelian Sovereignty) mostly act through **influence and downstream proxies** — Realm factions philosophically downstream without being the empire itself. The **Xyloryn** are the exception: they **invade**, and they are the **one threat the other empires fear openly.** Native nations (human kingdoms, elven powers, dwarves, druid collective, Horde-style collectives) are the board these powers play on.

## The Independent Powers — Hub Summaries

### Soulkeepers' Exchange → [`soulkeepers_exchange.md`](../factions/soulkeepers_exchange.md)

Cross-cosmic mercantile power dealing in continuity. **Not the client behind the six** — the **Nightbound** hired the Exchange; the **Broker** executed. Soul recall may be real; clean resurrection is the lie. Not casual resurrection merchants.

### N'hal → [`nhal.md`](../factions/nhal.md)

Ontological predators — not empire, swarm, or army. Denature reality; interface fails near them. Hidden threat while Xyloryn are the known apocalypse. Book 1 series-level only. Beatable only by impossible apex unity.

### The Nightbound → [`nightbound.md`](../factions/nightbound.md)

Seven-member interventionist order — female founder, evidentiary N'hal knowledge, six acquired as **keys**. Machiavellian anti-villains. Client above Broker.

### Realm Walkers → [`realmwalkers.md`](../factions/realmwalkers.md)

Loose category (~few dozen) — cross-realm witnesses. Book 1: drawn by Eriadne portal breach; contact Marcus post-Myrmidon. Foils to Nightbound. Riftwalker class ≠ faction.

## Scale, Apex Beings & the Ley Network

> The cast can defeat a **group-finale** threat (the Myrmidon). They do **not** defeat world-scale beings below.

### The Sea Dominion — water-aligned apex beings (a category)

**"Sea Dominion" is a *category of creature*, not a polity.** Leviathans, storm-dragons, oceanic apex entities as a class — not a nation, vessel, or single entity. Witnessed, survived, routed — never defeated. See `brent.md`, `master_timeline.md`.

### Scale hierarchy

| Tier | Example | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Cosmic trauma / impossible scale | archdemon / archangel (Xazzidiuk, Zazriel) | beings the story does not "beat" |
| Civilization-scale wonder | the **behemoth** (the Walking Grove) | a living world, not a boss |
| Apex ecological / mythic scale | **storm-dragon / leviathan (Sea Dominion)** | witnessed, survived, routed — not defeated |
| Local adventure-scale threat | a **dungeon** | the regional, beatable danger |
| **Group-finale threat** | the **Myrmidon** | the one the six can actually defeat — together |

### Eight Great Ley Lines & regional dungeons

**Eight Great Ley Lines**, one per foundational element (Fire, Water, Earth, Air/Wind, Light, Shadow, Life, Death). **Eriadne** sits at rare full/near-full convergence (`eriadne.md`). Branches and tributaries enable regional dungeons. Dungeons are ley-reactive pressure systems that can absorb local history and shift subtly with instability. See `serra_hawthorne.md`, `vessa.md`.

## Book-1 Story Hooks (where the cosmos touches the cast)

- **Marcus** — the **Concord** is the empire he'd admire first. The **Endless Reach** offers *certainty* (Meszkhal item). **Realm Walkers** recruit him.
- **Serra** — **Court of Seals** is regional limb of **Aurelian Sovereignty**; **Endless Reach** offers *total protective commitment.*
- **Seb** — **Soulkeepers' Exchange** through dead brother's soul; **Endless Reach** offers *salvation at any cost.*
- **Mara** — Xyloryn forms as readable fossils when swarm arrives.
- **Mathias** — detects Xyloryn/ruins-portal at Act-5 finale (injured, survives).
- **Cast as a whole** — escalation may be quiet trigger condition for **N'hal** (series-level Book 1).
