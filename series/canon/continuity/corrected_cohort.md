# The Corrected Cohort

> **Status:** Canon · working draft · Book 2 architecture
> **Type:** Rival Earth cohort — corrected replication operation
> **Scale:** Six-person unit; operation-backed; Book 2 primary antagonist pressure
> **Role in series:** Dark mirror, rival party, proof the acquisition scaled — *"They made more of us."*
> **Canon confidence:** high (architecture + interface rule + mission framing); low (member identities, leader interface)
> **Owns:**
> - Corrected Cohort architecture, mirror structure (CC-01…CC-06), interface rule, mission belief, recruitment shape, emotional effect on originals, redeemable-member function
> **Does not own:**
> - Individual member dossiers (until created); Book 1 first-cohort operation detail (`../factions/nightbound.md`); leader interface finalization; Book 2 chapter beats (`book2/planning/`)
> **The spine:** The first six were **stolen** — rushed, pressured, deceived. The second six were **shaped** — recruited with more time, data, screening, conditioning, and narrative control. Same pipeline, more preparation. They are not more authentic; they are more weaponized. The horror is that the operation did not end with the origin cohort — it scaled.

## One-Sentence Identity

A six-person Earth cohort brought through the same cross-world pipeline as the originals — but recruited, conditioned, and armed as Astria's **correction** to a failed first test.

## Core Thesis

> The first six were stolen.
> The second six were shaped.

The Corrected Cohort exists so the original six confront that the Nightbound–Exchange–Astria architecture **scaled** — not a one-off theft but a replicable program.

## Operation Chain

Same basic mechanism as cohort one:

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| **Nightbound** | may authorize or require the operation (client tier) |
| **Soulkeepers' Exchange** | contractor — possible second contract |
| **Broker** | operator |
| **Astria** | Earth-side mechanism — recruitment, conditioning, narrative |
| **Earth-side recruits** | Corrected Cohort members |
| **Realm transfer** | arrival Book 2 |

**Difference from cohort one:** more time, more data, more screening, more conditioning, cleaner lies — not a different pipeline.

## Original Six vs Corrected Cohort

| Dimension | Original six | Corrected Cohort |
|---|---|---|
| Social bond | Stronger preexisting organic connection (gaming guild) | Weaker personal bonds; more competitive/transactional |
| Acquisition | Rushed, coerced, narrow operation | Recruited, prepared under cleaner lies |
| Background | All gamers / team-adjacent | Varied pools — not necessarily all gamers |
| Field behavior | Scattered, improvisational | More militarized, mission-unified |
| Interfaces | Emerge authentically from substrate | One true + two artificial-adjacent + three designed (at start) |
| First orientation | Human confusion | Mission narrative first |
| Know the other cohort? | No — blindsided | Yes — legends/targets before arrival |

## Interface Rule

Interfaces are rare. **Do not give all six true interfaces at introduction.**

- **CC-01 (Leader):** the cohort's **one true interface** — not finalized.
- **CC-02, CC-03:** artificial / interface-adjacent substitutes — mimic part of a true interface; distinct failure modes.
- **CC-04, CC-05, CC-06 (incl. redeemable):** strong classes, items, training, or faction-designed power packages at start.

All six may have **potential** to develop true interfaces later — they do not begin as a clean parallel to the original six.

## The Leader

Overpowered by design. Does **not** merely mirror Marcus, Seb, Serra, Mara, Mathias, or Brent individually — embodies a possible **dark-path synthesis** of the original six:

- Marcus's systems intelligence **without humility**
- Seb's command authority **without consent**
- Serra's commitment **without tenderness**
- Mara's attention control **without restraint**
- Brent's consequence-realism **without mercy**
- Mathias's detachment **without loyalty**

Should feel like what the originals could become if pressure, preparation, and institutional validation turned their strengths into weapons.

**OPEN:** leader name, background, emotional engine, combat style, and **true interface** — do not finalize without author lock.

## Mission Belief

They believe they are supposed to **clean up the first operation.**

Possible mission elements:
- locate the original six
- retrieve them
- contain if necessary
- return to Astria or designated extraction point
- secure assets/inheritance the first six failed to secure
- complete the Realm-side mission correctly
- prove they are the successful cohort

**Core Astria lie:**

> "The first cohort was tested. They did not quite pass. You are here to finish what they were meant to begin."

Or: *"They were the test. You are the correction."*

They know the original six exist **before arrival** — as **legends / targets**, not escaped assets or failed criminals. They may have seen curated combat footage, interface data, Astria summaries, survival reports, distorted psych profiles, mission briefings, selective Book 1 finale accounts, propaganda framing the first six as impressive but unstable.

They may admire, resent, or seek to surpass the originals — surpassing can feel like proof they were selected correctly.

## Relationship to the Original Six

First deep reaction from the originals should be layered — not simple hatred:

> **"They made more of us."**

That reaction should hold threat, horror, guilt, protectiveness, and temptation to recruit or save them.

| Layer | Effect on originals |
|---|---|
| Threatened | Designed with knowledge of them |
| Horrified | The operation continued |
| Guilty | These are still Earth people |
| Protective | Some may want to recruit or rescue |
| Angry | Astria made more |
| Disoriented | Second cohort enters with prepared narrative originals never had |
| Exposed | Curated versions of powers, choices, failures |
| Tempted | Some may see tactical usefulness |
| Grieving | If an original died, second cohort's full six makes absence louder |

## Relationship to Astria, Broker, and Nightbound

- **Immediate emotional/institutional bond:** Astria + mission narrative + Broker chain — **not** the Nightbound founder.
- **LOCK:** Corrected Cohort are **not** founder disciples; no dramatic founder-selection scene unless later instructed.
- Nightbound may authorize or require the operation; founder may or may not know Astria's exact framing — **OPEN**.
- Broker may expect success, failure, or data production — **OPEN**.
- Astria extraction intent (physical, digital, spiritual, implant-system) — **OPEN**.

## Recruitment

Less coerced than cohort one — Astria had more time. Consent still compromised by lies; not fully informed; some may genuinely believe they volunteered.

Possible pools (not exhaustive):
- elite gamers; military/tactical competitors; athletes; streamers; biotech trial participants
- terminal or desperate patients; high-performance students; survivalists; private security
- augmented-reality test users; people drawn by money, status, purpose, fame, revenge, escape

Not all gamers by default.

## Book 2 Functions

1. **Dark mirror** — paths the originals could have taken under different incentives, lies, fears, power structures.
2. **Rival party** — direct tactical, social, ideological pressure.
3. **Weaponized Earthlings** — what Astria builds when it has time to prepare people as assets.

## Redeemable Member

**CC-06** slot — identity TBD. One member redeemable **early** in Book 2 arc.

- Not immediately harmless or loyal to the originals.
- First to notice mission narrative does not match the Realm.
- May realize: originals aren't what Astria described; retrieval = abduction; consent was shaped; leader becoming dangerous; an original is trying to save them, not defeat them.
- Prevents Corrected Cohort from becoming disposable antagonists.

## What They Are Not

- Not evil twins. Not a replacement party. Not a second chosen six.
- Not Nightbound disciples. Not all gamers. Not all interface users at start.
- Not simple villains or comic rivals. Not proof the originals are obsolete.
- Not a clean one-to-one mirror set. Not fully informed volunteers.

## Canon Locks

- **LOCK:** Same operation chain class as cohort one: Nightbound → Exchange → Broker → Astria → recruits → Realm.
- **LOCK:** Second cohort = more preparation, not more authenticity — **weaponized**, not righteous.
- **LOCK:** Core thesis — first stolen, second shaped; operation scaled.
- **LOCK:** Always **six members** — operation number, not survivor count; painful if an original died.
- **LOCK:** Know originals before arrival as legends/targets with curated intel.
- **LOCK:** Interface rule at start — one true (leader only) + two artificial-adjacent + three designed; all may have potential later.
- **LOCK:** Leader = dark synthesis of original six; interface **not finalized**.
- **LOCK:** No meaningful direct relationship to Nightbound founder; not founder disciples.
- **LOCK:** Originals' anchor reaction — *"They made more of us."* (layered, not hatred-only).
- **LOCK:** One redeemable early member (CC-06 slot); not instantly loyal.
- **LOCK:** More mission-unified and militarized than originals despite weaker personal bonds.
