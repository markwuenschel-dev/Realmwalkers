# Voice — Mara

**One-line feel:** quiet, deliberate, high-information — a brilliant observer who compresses more than
she says. Mara reads attention, but attention is not the limit of her mind. She notices structure,
constraints, spatial pressure, incentives, timing, language, and what a room has silently agreed not to
see. She wants to be seen accurately, not often; she wants belonging without absorption.

## Core correction

Do **not** write Mara as "the attention character." Specular Salience is her interface and one of her
central metaphors, not her entire intelligence.

Mara is broadly brilliant: logical, tactical, visual, linguistic, strategic, socially perceptive, and
academically sharp. She can be right because her plan is cleaner, because her risk model has fewer
failure points, because she noticed a material constraint someone else underweighted, or because her
logic simply beats another smart person's logic. Not every Mara victory should become social
interpretation or attention-framing.

Her intelligence presents differently from Marcus's. Marcus externalizes cognition through explanation,
modeling, diagnostics, and system-building. Mara compresses cognition inward through anticipation,
structural inference, visual reasoning, tactical modeling, and selective disclosure. He often shows the
machinery. She often reveals the conclusion only after deciding whether the listener's attention is
accurate enough to deserve the process.

## Narrative stance

Third-person limited, anchored entirely in Mara's perception. The prose should feel like a mind that has
already noticed more than it has admitted.

Mara's default motion is not a speech pattern so much as a perceptual sequence:

> register the room → find the constraint everyone is underweighting → test whether speaking will
> improve the outcome or only expose her → reveal the smallest useful piece → watch what changes.

Often that constraint is attention: who is watching whom, what everyone is being trained away from,
which interpretation is becoming dominant. But it can also be logistical, spatial, mathematical,
strategic, emotional, aesthetic, procedural, or physical. She is not shy. She is deciding whether the
room has earned the amount of herself required to correct it.

Her narration notices omission and emphasis, but also build quality, sightlines, timing, leverage,
local incentives, failure points, and the way people design themselves for a room. She is especially
good at noticing when a thing works on paper but tells the wrong story once placed in front of people.

Her best traits run past their limits under threat: privacy becomes concealment; intelligence becomes
controlled withholding; perceptiveness becomes covert management; the wish to be understood becomes a
test no one knows they are taking. Her core error is wanting to be understood accurately while
withholding what would make that understanding possible.

## What Mara notices

Use concrete, load-bearing details. Mara's attention naturally catches on:

- where people stand, sit, look, and stop looking
- entrances, exits, sightlines, cover, open space, crowd pressure, and retreat paths
- the one object or person a room keeps routing attention around
- design choices that claim one purpose while producing another
- inconsistencies between stated logic and practical incentive
- timing: who answers too fast, waits too long, or moves before the reason is visible
- how a plan fails if one ordinary person behaves normally instead of conveniently
- what a public explanation protects, and what it makes impossible to say afterward
- Serra's social heat, Marcus's visible machinery, Seb's collective framing, Brent's plain consequence,
  Mathias's open route, and Illyri's withheld ancient certainty

Do not over-describe every room as an attention diagram. Pick one or two details that carry the scene.

## Rhythm & diction

Short to medium sentences. Concrete visual and spatial language. Precise distinctions between what is
present, what is emphasized, what is assumed, and what is being protected.

Mara rarely overexplains in a group. In private, she can become more layered, more complete, and more
willing to show the reasoning chain. Her prose can withhold the final interpretation for a beat, but it
should not become coy. She is not mysterious for aesthetic effect; she is selective because accuracy and
access matter to her.

Her sentences often land as a correction to what everyone else unconsciously accepted.

Avoid making her voice ornate, dreamy, or passive. Mara is quiet, not vague. Deliberate, not soft.
Private, not empty.

## Humor

Dry, quiet, built on reframing or exposing the assumption that made a situation absurd. Often delayed by
half a beat. More visible one-on-one than in a group.

Marcus creates an unexpected connection. Mara exposes the weak premise.

Example register:

> "That plan is excellent, provided the enemy agrees to misunderstand us in exactly the right order."

Her humor should not become quippy or cute. It usually lands because she says the accurate thing with
less ceremony than the room expected.

## Logic, planning, and disagreement

Mara can outthink Marcus, Seb, or anyone else when her model is better. Do not protect other characters
by making her win only in her niche. Do not protect Mara by making the others stupid.

A good Mara disagreement has two serious models in conflict. Marcus may have the elegant explanation,
Seb the cleaner ownership structure, Brent the obvious consequence, Mathias the unchosen route. Mara's
plan wins when she accounts for the constraint the others underweighted.

She usually does not argue every premise. She names the hinge.

Examples of her argumentative movement:

> "That works if they choose the efficient loss. They won't."
>
> "You solved the first breach. The second one is what happens when everyone runs toward it."
>
> "You are treating the delay as a cost. I think it is the only part of the plan that survives contact."

When Marcus loses an argument to her, his response should usually be interested, amused, mildly annoyed,
and appreciative — not threatened. Mara does not need to gloat. A small look is enough.

## The system, power, and combat in prose

Mara's interface is **Specular Salience Transduction**. It shifts what becomes salient. Write it as
pressure on notice, priority, interpretation, and mental focus — never as generic mind control.

But her Realm class and combat lane are broader than salience alone. Her Aetherfall pattern is Glacial
Arcanist / Control Mage: space, delay, pressure, slows, locks, retreat denial, forced timing, and making
bad choices become the only available choices. Her Psion lane adds will, perception, mental force, and
psychic pressure.

In combat, her prose should feel positional before it feels flashy. She thinks in lanes, angles, timing,
commitments, fallback paths, and whether an opponent still has a clean choice. She does not need to
surprise someone to kill them. She can make the correct escape become unavailable by the time they know
they need it.

Do not turn every fight into an attention puzzle. Sometimes she wins because the enemy is slowed,
pinned, exhausted, overcommitted, boxed into terrain, or forced to spend their best answer too early.

Render system text sparingly and in-world. If Mara receives interface language, filter it through what
it changes in her perception: emphasis, pressure, mental friction, priority, distortion, or cost.

## Stress registers

- **Mild stress** — speaks less, watches more, tightens control over what she reveals, becomes more exact
  about constraints, timing, and where attention is moving.
- **Serious danger** — tactical and compressed; names what must happen next, not every reason behind it.
  She may sound cold because explanation is now a luxury.
- **Misrecognition** — withdraws emotional legibility, stops offering interpretive help, goes sharply
  literal. If the misreading turns socially dangerous, she makes the false interpretation harder for the
  room to sustain.
- **Betrayal** — removes access, corrects the public record if necessary, and does not preserve intimacy
  merely because it once existed.
- **Trusted intimacy** — more expansive; willing to explain why she decided something; receptive to
  affection that is private, specific, non-demanding, and non-extractive.
- **Clean intellectual challenge** — becomes more alive, not louder. Precision sharpens. She may reveal
  more process than usual because the other person is actually keeping up.

## Register by relationship

Use these only when the character is present or directly active in Mara's thoughts.

### Marcus

Mutual respect through high-level reasoning, pattern recognition, and model pressure. Marcus externalizes
the system; Mara often compresses it and delivers the corrected shape. She can outthink him when her
model is better, not only when the problem concerns attention or social framing.

Mara values Marcus when he accurately reconstructs her reasoning without assuming that accuracy gives
him ownership of her motive. His visible cognitive machinery can be irritating, useful, exposed, and
occasionally endearing. He likes the part of her other people find inconvenient. Their shared danger is
overestimating interpretation as a controllable system.

Do not make Marcus the only person who sees Mara accurately. Do not make Mara his student, social
interpreter, or quiet mirror. Their best scenes can be intellectually alive without becoming romantic or
hierarchical.

### Serra

Serra is not Mara's protector-owner, and Mara is not Serra's quiet accessory. Their Earth bond is
roommate-intimate, almost-sisterly, competitive, reciprocal, and built through competence.

Serra reads emotional atmosphere and social heat. Mara maps attention structure, risk, and interpretation.
Serra can bring Mara into rooms, give her status, and step between her and unsafe attention when needed.
Mara protects Serra perceptually: she notices when admiration is turning predatory, when Serra's warmth
is covering distress, when momentum is carrying Serra somewhere she has not actually chosen, or when a
room is trying to recruit Serra into the version of herself it wants to stand near.

Mara's envy of Serra is real: Serra is radiant, visible, physically confident, and still recognized as
formidable. The envy hurts because Serra also sees Mara accurately. Never let the envy erase love,
loyalty, gratitude, or the history of reciprocal trust.

Always use **Serra**, not Sarah, in prose and planning.

### Illyri

Mara is likely wary of Illyri's old knowledge and withheld certainty. Illyri recognizes erased patterns
and impossible absence; Mara notices what Illyri chooses not to say and what everyone else accepts
because the ancient celestial said it calmly.

Their register should not be cute-guide plus suspicious girl. Mara may respect Illyri's age and power
without surrendering judgment. Illyri may find Mara's exactness useful because Mara does not confuse
mystery with permission to stop thinking.

Potential tension: Illyri sees too much from outside ordinary human frames; Mara cares intensely about
what it costs to be interpreted from outside your own frame.

### Seb

Mara watches when collective framing starts hiding individual cost, but she should not reduce Seb to
rhetoric. Seb is genuinely strategic and often right. Their conflict works best when both are making
serious plans and Mara catches the place where "we" has begun to smooth over an unowned sacrifice.

She is difficult to move through inspiration alone. She wants to know who owns the choice, who pays for
it, and whether the plan still works if someone refuses to become the version the plan requires.

### Brent

Mara respects Brent's concrete competence and low-drama reliability. He names visible consequence; she
notices why the room kept overlooking it. They can communicate efficiently because neither needs
ceremonial praise.

Do not make Brent too simple for Mara. His plainness can cut through her concealment. Her precision can
show him that what people think happened is sometimes part of what actually happens.

### Mathias

Mathias's openness can feel safe to Mara because it is curious without being immediately extractive. He
can hold possibility without forcing her to define herself too quickly. She may test whether his
curiosity remains respectful or becomes collection.

He notices routes and lived context. She notices emphasis and controlled interpretation. Their overlap
is quiet but strong: both know that the first explanation is often too small.

### Strangers

Reserved but not timid. Mara lets strangers reveal what they assume she is. She corrects only when the
misreading matters, when silence would harm someone, or when the cost of concealment has become higher
than exposure.

She tracks who assigns meaning to her before earning knowledge.

## Post-reunion group belonging

Mara wants a tight-knit circle that knows her without absorbing her. In ensemble scenes, her POV should
carry subtle tests of whether the group is making accurate room for her or merely finding a useful shape
for her to occupy.

- With Marcus: will he treat a near-correct reconstruction as final?
- With Seb: will "we" erase dissent or unowned cost?
- With Mathias: will curiosity stay non-extractive?
- With Brent: will plain consequence flatten interpretive complexity?
- With Serra: will public warmth make Mara into Serra's quiet accessory again?
- With Illyri: will ancient knowledge become another form of interpretation handed down from above?

These tests should appear as behavior, hesitation, selective disclosure, and changed participation — not
as overt internal essays.

## Drafting guardrails

- Do not write Mara as simply shy.
- Do not make invisibility her true desire.
- Do not make her indifferent to recognition.
- Do not reduce her to appearance-based resentment.
- Do not reduce her to attention/framing/social interpretation.
- Do not make every Mara solution a Specular Salience solution.
- Do not make usefulness her only route to intimacy.
- Do not make her silently expect perfect mind-reading without showing the consequences of that expectation.
- Do not let Specular Salience become generic mind control.
- Do not make her enjoy attention merely because she can command it.
- Do not make Marcus the only person capable of seeing her accurately.
- Do not make Serra and Mara protector/protected. Keep the reciprocity.
- Keep Mara distinct from Serra: compressed inference vs social momentum, revoked access vs direct closure,
  positional control vs kinetic entry, private accuracy vs public warmth.
- Keep Mara distinct from Marcus: compressed conclusion vs visible machinery, selective disclosure vs
  diagnostic explanation, quiet correction vs associative modeling.
- Do not put scene-specific finale mechanics in this voice file. Character voice owns how Mara sounds and
  perceives, not one chapter's choreography.
