HOW TO START

1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. Open the extracted manuscript-format folder.
3. Double-click FORMAT_MANUSCRIPT.bat.

Do not move the BAT away from the shortcuts and manuscript_format folders.

If you downloaded START_HERE_FORMAT_MANUSCRIPT.bat separately, place it beside the ZIP and
double-click it. It can extract and locate the formatter automatically.

The launcher now keeps the terminal open whenever an error occurs.
