# Chapter 1 — The Channel
<!-- chapter number=1 kind=chapter title="The Channel" pov="Wren" -->

<!-- scene index=1 scene_no=1 -->

The sluice gate groaned open on hinges older than the numbers stamped into them.

```
@interface role=system
You have entered the **Glass Aqueduct of Vellis**. Ambient mana density is high;
your corruption resistance is being tested.
```

She had read about ambient mana density. Reading about it had not prepared her for
the way it tasted — like a coin held too long under the tongue.

```
@interface domain=fire skill="Sparkwhip" tier=III
A whip of living flame that hungers for what it touches; each strike deepens the burn.
```

<!-- scene index=2 scene_no=2 -->

The thing in the cistern had been waiting a long time, and it had learned patience the
way stone learns it.

```
@interface creature=monster domain=blood intensity=strong skill="Hollow Queen"
A bloated arachnid matriarch whose egg-sacs glow with borrowed light. She does not
chase — she waits, and the channel does the chasing for her.
```

> [!WARNING]
> The aqueduct does not forgive a second mistake.

# Chapter 2 — What It Cost
<!-- chapter number=2 kind=chapter title="What It Cost" pov="Wren" -->

<!-- scene index=1 scene_no=1 -->

She came up out of the cistern three days older and one marker short.

```
@interface role=levelup name="Wren Calloway" from=6 to=7
You have reached **Level 7**. As a Human, your ambition outpaces your years —
you gain **4 free attribute points** to spend. Choose wisely.
- Health: 72 -> 84
- Mana: 37 -> 45
- Stamina: 55 -> 60
- Corruption: 12 -> 31
```

```
@interface role=skill domain=fire skill="Sparkwhip" tier=I rank="Novice" via="200 successful casts under strain"
Repetition has taught your hand what instruction never could — the flame now
answers as an extension of your will.
```

# Epilogue
<!-- chapter kind=epilogue pov="Wren" -->

<!-- scene index=1 scene_no=1 -->

The gate closed behind her. It did not latch. That was the part she would think about
later, on the nights she could not sleep.
