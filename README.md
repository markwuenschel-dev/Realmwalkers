# Dominion Realm

**Interface fantasy disguised as LitRPG.**

Six elite gamers receive neuroquantum implants and wake up in a world that isn't a game. The Dominion Realm is real — metaphysical, dangerous, indifferent to their UI. When the guild fractures and each member is changed by separate paths, Marcus must survive long enough to learn whether the people he lost are still people he belongs with.

## The Series

*Dominion Realm* is an ongoing novel series. Book 1 follows six people scattered across a world they were never meant to enter, changed by trajectories that pull in different directions, and forced to reckon with a question the game never asked them: not whether they can survive, but whether they still belong together.

The magic system is one substrate — Aether, the Prime Force — accessed through radically different personal interfaces. The UI is an implant translating metaphysical law into game logic. The Realm predates the UI by centuries. Every visible threat is a limb of something larger and not yet seeable.
